### Not started — outbound calling

Triggering a call the moment a booking form comes in. **Blocked on compliance:**

The FCC ruled in Feb 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial voice" under the TCPA. In California that means prior express written consent before calling a cell. **The booking form has no consent checkbox.** One must be added, consent must be stored with the lead, and only leads who consented under the new language can be called.

The technical side is easy — `netlify/functions/submission-created.js` already fires on every submission and already parses name, phone, vehicle, dates, and message. It's ~15 lines to add the call trigger. Do not build it before consent is live.

Also flagged: the website FAQ says personal auto insurance *"typically extends to rental vehicles, including exotic rentals."* Given what Ali said about Geico/Progressive/Mercury, that's more confident than reality and should be softened.
