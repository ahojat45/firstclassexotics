## 2. ⚠️ TWO CONVENTIONS CHANGED TODAY — read before editing anything

### Every image needs a `.webp` sibling and a `<picture>` wrapper

```html
<picture><source srcset="path/name-01.webp" type="image/webp"><img width="800" height="533" loading="lazy" src="path/name-01.jpg" alt="..." data-gallery="..." onerror="this.style.background='#1C1C1C'"></picture>
```

WebP at **quality 82, identical pixel dimensions** to the JPEG. Never upscale — if the
source is smaller than 1600px, leave it (see §6). 123 `.webp` files exist now.

### Internal links must point at the SERVED URL, never `.html`

`href="/gift"` not `href="gift.html"`. `href="/blog/<slug>"` not `href="blog-<slug>.html"`.
From a `/blog/` page, `href="/"` not `href="../index.html"`.

This is the same rule that already applied to canonical, og:url and sitemap — it now
applies to `href` too. **1,029 links violated it until today.** If a generator or the
Blog Publisher reintroduces `.html` hrefs, that is a regression.

Never rewrite these five: `404.html`, `blog-publisher.html`, `agreement.html`,
`google49d716d460298fda.html`, `google8cf0ce3903a867e7.html`.

roughly (770, 33) on a 1501px-wide window, typing, pressing Return, then clicking the
button **via JS** rather than by coordinate:

```js

  .filter(e=>e.childElementCount===0 && /^REQUEST INDEXING$/i.test((e.textContent||'').trim()))[0].click()
```

**Keep this in proportion.** Request Indexing is a nudge. The durable fix is the
internal-link rewrite in §1 — that is what gets the other ~40 pages crawled without

   The grep searched for the filename.

   `/blog/lamborghini-temerario-rental-california`. Reverted in `44caaf1`.

**Rule: before calling any page dead or missing, follow the redirect chain to the end and
check what the URL actually returns.** `netlify.toml` has 80 rules, including 200-rewrites
that make root files serve at nested URLs.
