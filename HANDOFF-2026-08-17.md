## 2. ⚠️ CONVENTIONS — read before editing anything

### Every image needs a `.webp` sibling and a `<picture>` wrapper

```html
<picture><source srcset="path/name-01.webp" type="image/webp"><img width="800" height="533" loading="lazy" src="path/name-01.jpg" alt="..." data-gallery="..." onerror="this.style.background='#1C1C1C'"></picture>
```

WebP at **quality 82, identical pixel dimensions**. Never upscale — if the source is under
1600px, leave it. **The rule covers `data-gallery` too, not just `src`.** The homepage
lightbox calls `webpUrl()` and requests `.webp` first, falling back to JPEG on error — a new
car added without `.webp` still displays, just heavier. Adding them is not optional; the
fallback prevents a broken gallery, it does not excuse skipping the step.

### Internal links must point at the SERVED URL, never `.html`

`href="/gift"` not `href="gift.html"`. `href="/blog/<slug>"` not `href="blog-<slug>.html"`.
From a `/blog/` page, `href="/"` not `href="../index.html"`.

Same rule already applied to canonical, og:url and sitemap. **1,029 links violated it until
13 Aug.** If a generator reintroduces `.html` hrefs, that is a regression.

Never rewrite these five: `404.html`, `blog-publisher.html`, `agreement.html`,
`google49d716d460298fda.html`, `google8cf0ce3903a867e7.html`.

### Review count changes in two places, together

`"reviewCount"` in the JSON-LD (index.html ~line 78) and the visible
`"N Google reviews"` text (~line 1937). **Never one without the other.**

## 8. Mistakes made — do not repeat

### 8a. Judging a page by its filename instead of its served URL (13 Aug)

Three misreads, same root cause. The 8 root-level `blog-*.html` files are **live posts**
served at `/blog/<slug>` via **status-200 rewrites** at `netlify.toml` ~line 146.
`/blog/lamborghini-temerario-the-huracan-era-is-ending` is a **retired duplicate** that 301s
to `/blog/lamborghini-temerario-rental-california`.

**Rule: before calling any page dead or missing, follow the redirect chain to the end and
check what the URL actually returns.** `netlify.toml` has 80 rules, first-match-wins,
including 200-rewrites that make root files serve at nested URLs.

### 8b. Insurance wording — RESOLVED 14 Aug (`f99e085`, `cdd2477`)

Old text asserted a customer's coverage: *"Your existing full coverage personal auto
insurance typically extends to rental vehicles, including exotic rentals."* Found in **three**
places — the homepage visible FAQ **and the JSON-LD schema** (so Google could surface the
claim directly), plus a softer variant on `blog.html`.

Fixed by making **no claim at all** about the customer's policy, then adding a real
operational fact Ali confirmed: **FCE calls the customer's insurer at time of booking,
verifies the policy, and confirms the specific vehicle is covered — every rental, without
exception.** Live text now:

> Insurance requirements depend on your own policy, and coverage for high-value vehicles
> varies widely between insurers. You are not left to figure it out alone: at the time of
> booking we call your insurer to verify your policy and confirm the specific vehicle you are
> renting is covered. We do this on every rental, without exception. If your existing policy
> qualifies, we do not require you to purchase additional coverage. If it does not, we will
> go over your options with you before anything is confirmed.

⚠️ **"every rental, without exception" is now a public promise.** Ali confirmed it is
absolute. If that ever becomes "usually", soften it — an unkept promise is worse than vague.

### 8c. Blog Publisher security — FIXED 14 Aug (`6bb5b0a`)

`blog-publisher.html` shipped `const PASSWORD='FCE2026'` to every visitor and the Netlify
function compared against the same hardcoded literal. The page is public, so anyone viewing
source had a working credential to publish to the live site. Abuse check found none.
`GITHUB_TOKEN` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` were always env vars and were never exposed.

**Now:** password lives only in Netlify env var **`BLOG_PUBLISHER_PASSWORD`** (Production
scope, marked secret, Functions scope included). Server **fails closed** with 500 if unset —
no fallback, and none should ever be added. Compare is `crypto.timingSafeEqual`. The client
holds nothing: operator types it, held in `sessionStorage` for that tab only, login verifies
via `operation:'auth'`, logout clears it. Page carries `noindex`.

⚠️ **If the publisher returns 500 "BLOG_PUBLISHER_PASSWORD not configured", the env var was
deleted or its scope changed. Re-add it in Netlify — do NOT reintroduce a hardcoded
default.** The old password is in git history forever and must never be reused.

**Extensionless hrefs (the other half of that item): CONFIRMED GOOD.** The function strips
`.html` from canonical and `og:url` and rewrites blog-card hrefs before committing.
`blog/<slug>.html` appears only as the on-disk path, which is correct.
