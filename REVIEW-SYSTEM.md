## Rules that keep you out of trouble

These matter. Violating them can get reviews deleted or the profile penalized.

**Never offer anything in exchange for a review.** No discount, no free upgrade, no entry
into a drawing. This violates Google's policy outright. It is also the most common way
small businesses lose their reviews in bulk.

**Never ask only the happy customers.** Screening people — asking who's satisfied first and
only sending the link to those who say yes — is called review gating and it is against
Google's policy. Ask everyone, the same way.

**Never write them yourself or have staff write them.** Google detects this reliably.

**Never bulk-send from one device on one wifi.** Space them out.

## Responding to reviews — do not skip this

You have **4 unread reviews** right now. Response rate is a ranking signal, and prospects
read your replies while deciding between you and Mango.

**Reply to every review within 24 hours.** Reference their specific car or occasion —
generic "Thanks for the 5 stars!" replies add nothing.

> Thank you [Name]! The [car] was a great choice for [occasion] — glad it delivered.
> Come see us next time you need something special.

**If you get a negative one:** reply calm, brief, factual, and take it offline. You are not
writing to the reviewer — you are writing to the next 50 people who read it. Never argue
in public. One bad review answered well does less damage than a defensive reply.
