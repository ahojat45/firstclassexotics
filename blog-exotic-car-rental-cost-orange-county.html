How Much Does It Cost to Rent an
Exotic Car in Orange County?

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  6 min read  ·  Updated May 2026

One of the most common questions we receive at First Class Exotics is: “How much does it actually cost to rent a Lamborghini or Ferrari in Orange County?” The honest answer depends on the vehicle, the duration of your rental, the day of the week, and whether you are adding delivery or other options. But we believe in transparency, so here is a thorough breakdown of everything that goes into the cost of an exotic car rental in OC — with no surprises.

What Is Included in the Daily Rate

At First Class Exotics, our pricing is transparent. There are no hidden fees buried in the fine print. Your daily rate includes use of the vehicle, a base mileage allowance (typically 100–200 miles per day depending on the car), and our personal concierge support throughout your rental. We are available by phone throughout your rental period for any questions, directions, or assistance you need.

All vehicles are delivered fully detailed, fueled, and ready for your day. We walk you through every feature of the car at delivery so you feel completely comfortable before you drive away. That personal touch is something you will not find with large corporate rental companies — and it is part of what our clients come back for year after year.

What Costs Extra

Being fully informed before you book is important, so here is what may be added to the base rate:

  • Delivery fees: Delivery to your hotel or address within OC is available for a flat fee based on distance from our Costa Mesa location. Fees for LA, San Diego, and Riverside County are disclosed upfront with your quote.
  • Excess mileage: Miles driven beyond your daily allowance are charged at a per-mile rate specified in your rental agreement. If you plan an extended drive — like a day trip to Palm Springs or a weekend in Santa Barbara — let our team know so we can build the right mileage package.
  • Additional day charges: If you keep the vehicle longer than booked, we will accommodate when possible and charge the standard daily rate for each additional day.
  • Cleaning fees: Vehicles returned in excessively dirty condition (beyond normal use) may incur a detailing fee. This almost never applies to our normal clientele.

The Security Deposit

A security deposit hold is placed on your credit card at the time of vehicle delivery. This is a temporary hold, not a charge — it is released in full when the vehicle is returned in the same condition. The deposit amount varies by vehicle and will be clearly disclosed when you book. It ranges from approximately $2,500 for entry-level exotics to $10,000+ for our most exclusive vehicles. The deposit protects both parties and is standard practice across the industry.

Your credit card must have sufficient available credit to accommodate the hold in addition to your rental payment. Plan accordingly when choosing which card to use.

Insurance and Coverage

Your personal auto insurance is your primary coverage for an exotic rental. Before you book, call your insurance agent and ask specifically whether your policy covers high-value rental vehicles. Most comprehensive personal auto policies do extend coverage to rentals, but coverage limits vary and some policies have exclusions for vehicles above a certain value. This one phone call can save you significant cost and stress.

If your personal policy does not provide adequate coverage, we can discuss supplemental options when you contact us. Our team has helped hundreds of clients navigate this and we will guide you to the right solution for your situation.

How to Get the Best Deal

The single most effective way to reduce your effective per-day cost is to rent for multiple days. Our multi-day discounts are meaningful — a 3-day rental can reduce your per-day cost by 15–20%, and a week-long rental by 25–35% depending on the vehicle. If you are planning a weekend in SoCal, a 2-night rental almost always makes more financial sense than two separate 1-day rentals.

  • Weekday vs. weekend: Weekday (Monday–Thursday) rentals often carry lower rates than peak weekend demand periods
  • Book in advance: Last-minute bookings for specific, popular vehicles can carry premium pricing due to limited availability
  • Be flexible on model: If you are open to similar vehicles in the same category, our team may have a comparable car available at a lower rate
  • Ask about packages: For special occasions like birthdays or anniversaries, we occasionally offer experiential packages that combine vehicles and extras at a bundled rate

Is It Worth It?

After 12 years of doing this, here is what we have observed: clients who rent exotics almost universally describe it as one of the best experiences of their year. These are not cars you can easily access any other way — and experiencing one on the right roads, on the right day, in Southern California is genuinely extraordinary. The cost, when divided against the depth of the experience, is one that most clients repeat as often as they can.
