How to Rent an Exotic Car for
Your Wedding in SoCal

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  5 min read  ·  Updated May 2026

Your wedding day is one of the most important days of your life, and the details you choose — the venue, the flowers, the dress, the car — become part of the story you tell for decades. A Rolls-Royce Ghost, a gleaming Ferrari California, or a pristine white Bentley Continental GT as your wedding car creates an entrance that photographs capture forever. Here is everything you need to plan the perfect exotic wedding car rental in Orange County or Los Angeles.

Most Popular Wedding Cars

Not every exotic is right for a wedding. The best wedding cars balance visual impact with elegance and practicality. Here are the vehicles our clients choose most often:

  • Rolls-Royce Ghost: The undisputed classic choice. Timeless, silent, and unmistakably luxurious. The Ghost seats four adults in supreme comfort and arrives at any venue with an air of authority that no other vehicle can replicate. Perfect for traditional, formal ceremonies.
  • Rolls-Royce Cullinan: For the couple who wants the entire family in one vehicle, or a grander, more commanding entrance. The Cullinan is the pinnacle of automotive luxury in SUV form — and it photographs beautifully against any backdrop.
  • Bentley Continental GT: A sportier, slightly more personal expression of British luxury. Stunning in pearl white, silver, or midnight blue, the Continental GT is the choice for couples who want elegance with a touch of personality.
  • Ferrari California / F8 Spider: For the couple that has always wanted to arrive in a Ferrari. The retractable hardtop means you can choose open or closed depending on weather and preference, and the prancing horse on the hood says everything about your taste.
  • Lamborghini Huracán: For the boldest, most unforgettable entrance possible. If your wedding has energy, personality, and a guest list that appreciates the extraordinary, arriving in a Huracán is a moment your guests will talk about for years.

Book Early — Very Early

Wedding dates are fixed and absolutely non-negotiable. Our most popular vehicles — especially the Rolls-Royce Ghost and Cullinan — are booked months in advance for peak wedding season. For popular SoCal wedding dates (particularly June, September, and October weekends), we recommend securing your vehicle 3–6 months in advance. For specific holiday weekends or venues with strict timing requirements, earlier is always better.

Early booking also gives our team time to prepare the vehicle to an even higher standard for your special day — fresh detail, fresh flowers if requested, and any specific finishing touches you have in mind.

Popular Wedding Venues We Serve

Our family team has delivered to virtually every major wedding venue in Orange County and Los Angeles. Some of our most frequent destinations include:

  • The Resort at Pelican Hill — Newport Coast (arrival through the rotunda is extraordinary in a Rolls-Royce)
  • Montage Laguna Beach — Laguna Beach (clifftop setting, absolutely stunning in photos)
  • Ritz-Carlton Laguna Niguel — Dana Point (ocean-view terrace, classic OC luxury)
  • Waldorf Astoria Monarch Beach — Dana Point (resort-style elegance on the Pacific)
  • Balboa Bay Resort — Newport Beach (waterfront venue on the Newport Harbor)
  • Bel-Air Bay Club — Pacific Palisades (private, exclusive, ocean views)
  • Greystone Mansion — Beverly Hills (the most iconic estate venue in LA)

How to Coordinate with Your Photographer

The car is part of your wedding photography — treat it that way. Tell your photographer which vehicle you have booked as early as possible so they can plan their shot list around it. The best wedding car photos come from a combination of exterior arrival shots, couple portraits with the car, and interior detail shots of the cabin.

Let our team know if you have specific photo locations in mind beyond the venue. For example, many Orange County couples stop at Crystal Cove bluffs or Pelican Hill overlooks on the way to or from the venue. We will build extra time into the delivery window to accommodate those stops if you want them.

What Our Family Team Handles

From delivery to the getting-ready location or venue, coordination with your wedding planner on timing, to return pickup at the end of the evening — our family team handles every logistical detail so you can stay completely focused on your day. We stay in contact with your planner throughout the day and adjust timing if the ceremony or reception runs early or late. This is a service that impersonal rental companies simply cannot provide.

We can also arrange multiple vehicles if you have a bridal party who wants to arrive in style, or if you want both a classic vehicle for the ceremony and something more spirited for your exit. Our team will build the right package for your vision.

Day-Of Checklist

  • Confirm delivery time and address with our team 48 hours in advance
  • Share the wedding day timeline with us so we understand all timing constraints
  • If wearing a gown, consider asking us about interior protection options for long trains
  • Designate one person (often the wedding planner or day-of coordinator) as the contact for our team
  • Confirm return pickup location and time — this is sometimes different from the delivery location

Planning a wedding in Orange County or Los Angeles?
Tell our family team your date, venue, and vision. We will help you select the perfect car and coordinate everything around your most important day. Couples who work with us describe the experience as effortless — that is exactly what we aim for.
