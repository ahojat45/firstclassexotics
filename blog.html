Best Roads to Drive a Lamborghini
in Orange County

By Ali Hojat, Founder — First Class Exotics  ·  8 min read  ·  Updated May 2025

Orange County is one of the most underrated driving destinations in all of California. While most people think of LA when they imagine exotic cars, OC has something LA cannot offer: clean roads, ocean views, and enough open stretches to actually feel what these machines can do. After more than a decade of delivering Lamborghinis, Ferraris, and McLarens across Southern California, our family team has driven every road worth driving. Here are the best.

1. Pacific Coast Highway (PCH) — Dana Point to Malibu

There is no more iconic road to drive an exotic in California than PCH. Starting in Dana Point and heading north through Laguna Beach, Newport Beach, and eventually into Malibu, this coastal stretch offers ocean views on one side and California lifestyle on the other. The road is not particularly technical, but it is scenic beyond belief and guaranteed to turn every head on the route.

Best time to drive: Early Sunday morning before 8am. The road is quiet, the light is golden, and you will have the PCH almost entirely to yourself. Avoid weekend afternoons when traffic through Laguna Beach can bring even a Lamborghini to a crawl.

Pro tip from Ali: Start in Dana Point at the harbor, grab a coffee, then cruise north. Stop at Crystal Cove for photos — the bluffs behind a Huracán or Ferrari F8 make for some of the best content you will ever post.

2. Ortega Highway (SR-74) — Lake Elsinore to San Juan Capistrano

If PCH is the scenic drive, Ortega Highway is the driving drive. This mountain road connecting San Juan Capistrano to Lake Elsinore is packed with sweeping curves, elevation changes, and stunning canyon views that let you experience what an exotic car was actually engineered for. The road climbs into the Santa Ana Mountains before descending into the Inland Valley.

Best time to drive: Weekday mornings. This road attracts motorcyclists and enthusiasts on weekends, so midweek is cleaner. Check for road conditions after rain — the road can have debris after winter storms.

Caution: This is an active mountain road with real curves. Respect the speed limits, especially in a high-powered exotic you may not be familiar with. Always start slower than you think you need to.

3. Newport Coast Drive

This is the prestige drive. Newport Coast Drive winds through some of the most expensive real estate in Orange County, offering panoramic Pacific Ocean views and beautifully maintained roads. It connects PCH to the Pelican Hill area and Crystal Cove, and it is the kind of road where a Rolls-Royce Ghost or Bentley Continental feels perfectly at home.

The road is also conveniently close to excellent dining — the Pelican Hill Resort, The Ranch, and Crystal Cove Shake Shack are all along or near the route. Park the exotic, grab lunch, and enjoy the view before heading back out.

4. Santiago Canyon Road

Less trafficked than Ortega, Santiago Canyon Road runs through the foothills of Orange County connecting Irvine to the canyon areas near Orange and Anaheim Hills. It is one of the few roads in OC where you genuinely feel like you have escaped the city while still being minutes from everything. Rolling hills, ranches, and almost no traffic on weekday mornings make this a hidden gem for exotic drivers.

5. Crown Valley Parkway to Laguna Niguel

Crown Valley is one of the best-maintained boulevards in South Orange County. Wide lanes, clean pavement, and beautifully landscaped medians make it a joy to cruise in any exotic. It connects the 5 freeway to the Laguna Niguel hillside communities, ending near the Ritz-Carlton Laguna Niguel — a perfect stop for a photo with your rental.

Our Recommended OC Exotic Day Route

Start your day with a pickup from our Costa Mesa location. Head south on PCH through Laguna Beach to Dana Point for breakfast at The Fisherman’s Restaurant. Then loop back through Newport Coast Drive for photos at Crystal Cove. Take MacArthur through Newport to Fashion Island for a midday cruise through one of OC’s most scenic shopping districts. Finish with a sunset drive north on PCH back toward Newport Beach and Huntington Beach.

Total drive time: 3–4 hours of actual driving. Memories: forever.

Lamborghini vs Ferrari:
Which Should You Rent in SoCal?

By Ali Hojat, Founder — First Class Exotics  ·  7 min read  ·  Updated May 2025

After more than a decade of delivering both Lamborghinis and Ferraris to clients across Orange County, Los Angeles, and San Diego, we have heard the same question thousands of times: “Which one should I get?” The honest answer is that they are both extraordinary — but they are extraordinary in completely different ways. Here is how to choose the right one for your day.

The Personality Difference

Before we get into specs and price, understand this: Lamborghini and Ferrari represent two different philosophies about what a car should be. Lamborghini is theater. It is aggression, spectacle, and drama. Ferrari is precision. It is a sports car first, a statement second. Both are world-class. Neither is wrong. But one will suit your day better than the other.

Lamborghini Huracán EVO — For Drama, Attention and Raw Excitement

The Huracán is the car people stop their car to take a photo of. Its aggressive wedge shape, dramatic scissor door design on some variants, and the sound of its naturally aspirated V10 engine are designed to make a statement at every traffic light, every valet stand, and every stoplight on PCH.

  • 640 horsepower naturally aspirated V10
  • 0–60 in under 2.9 seconds
  • Available in Spyder (convertible) for the ultimate open-air experience
  • Polarizing, aggressive design that generates maximum attention
  • Wider, more planted feel on canyon roads

Best for: Birthdays, special occasions, social media content, events where you want to make the biggest possible impression. The Urus SUV version is also perfect for groups of up to 4 who want the Lamborghini experience with more practicality.

Ferrari F8 Spider — For the Pure Driver’s Experience

The F8 Spider is a masterpiece of engineering refinement. Where the Lamborghini screams, the Ferrari whispers with authority. Its twin-turbocharged V8 produces 710 horsepower and delivers one of the most communicative, precise driving experiences available at any price point. On canyon roads, it is simply magical.

  • 710 horsepower twin-turbo V8
  • 0–60 in 2.8 seconds
  • Convertible Spider roof that retracts in 14 seconds
  • More compact and nimble than the Lamborghini through tight corners
  • Ferrari’s legendary steering feel and feedback

Best for: Driving enthusiasts who want a car that rewards skill and input. If you are going to spend real time on Ortega Highway or canyon roads, the Ferrari is the more engaging car of the two. It also tends to attract slightly more discerning admirers than the Lamborghini.

Head-to-Head Summary

Choose the Lamborghini if: you want maximum attention, you are celebrating something big, you want the most recognizable silhouette in the world, or you simply want to feel like a superstar every time you pull up somewhere.

Choose the Ferrari if: you are a driving enthusiast who wants the car to respond to your inputs, you value precision over spectacle, or you want a car that serious car people will respect as much as casual onlookers admire it.

Cannot decide? Rent one for the first day and ask our family team about adding a second car to your booking at a discounted rate. We make it easy to experience both in the same trip.

The Complete Guide to Renting an
Exotic Car in Los Angeles

By Ali Hojat, Founder — First Class Exotics  ·  9 min read  ·  Updated May 2025

Los Angeles is one of the greatest cities in the world to drive an exotic car. The car culture runs deep, the roads range from legendary canyon passes to iconic coastal boulevards, and nowhere else on earth will you pull up to a valet in a Lamborghini and feel more at home. Here is everything you need to know to make your LA exotic rental perfect.

Best Neighborhoods to Be Seen

Beverly Hills: Rodeo Drive, Wilshire Boulevard, and the surrounding residential streets are the heart of LA luxury car culture. Valets here see everything — but a Lamborghini or Rolls-Royce still turns heads. Perfect for dinners, shopping, or hotel arrivals at the Peninsula or Beverly Wilshire.

West Hollywood: Sunset Strip and Melrose Avenue are high-energy, high-visibility, and perfect for a vibrant Saturday night arrival. The energy here matches a Lamborghini perfectly.

Malibu: For pure natural beauty and PCH driving, nothing beats Malibu. The stretch from Santa Monica to Point Dume is one of the most beautiful coastal drives in the world, and the Nobu Malibu valet with an exotic makes for unforgettable photos.

Calabasas: The epicenter of luxury SUV culture in LA. The Lamborghini Urus is practically a neighborhood car here, and showing up in one feels completely natural.

Best Drives in the LA Area

Mulholland Drive: The legendary canyon road running along the Santa Monica Mountains above the city. Winding, dramatic, and offering jaw-dropping views of both the San Fernando Valley and the LA Basin. Best driven on a clear weekday morning before traffic builds up.

Malibu Canyon Road: Connects PCH to Agoura Hills through the Santa Monica Mountains. Technical, scenic, and relatively quiet. One of the best driving roads in all of Southern California.

Angeles Crest Highway: For a more adventurous drive, Angeles Crest climbs into the San Gabriel Mountains above Pasadena. The scenery is breathtaking and the road is serious enough to truly appreciate what an exotic can do.

When to Drive — Timing is Everything in LA

Traffic in LA is real, and there is nothing worse than sitting in gridlock on the 405 in a Lamborghini. Here is how to plan your timing:

  • Canyon drives: Weekday mornings 7–10am are perfect
  • Beverly Hills cruising: Saturday or Sunday 10am–1pm before lunch crowds
  • PCH to Malibu: Early Sunday morning is magical — minimal traffic, golden hour light
  • Sunset Strip: Thursday–Saturday evenings after 7pm when the energy is at its peak
  • Avoid: Friday afternoon 2–7pm on any freeway. You will regret it.

Renting an Exotic Car for the First Time?
Read This First.

By Ali Hojat, Founder — First Class Exotics  ·  8 min read  ·  Updated May 2025

Renting an exotic car for the first time is genuinely one of the most exciting experiences you can have. But going in without preparation can turn excitement into stress. After 10+ years of walking first-time renters through the process, here is everything our family team wishes everyone knew before their first rental.

1. Understand Your Insurance

This is the most important step. Check with your personal auto insurance provider before you rent. Many standard policies extend coverage to rental vehicles, including exotic rentals. Call your agent and ask specifically about high-value rental vehicles. If you are not covered, we can discuss options when you book.

2. Give Yourself More Time Than You Think You Need

Picking up an exotic car for the first time is not like picking up a regular rental. We walk you through the controls, go over the car together, answer all your questions, and make sure you feel completely comfortable before you leave. Budget an extra 30 minutes for this — it is worth it.

3. Start Slow — Seriously

A Lamborghini or Ferrari has more power than most people have ever experienced. The first 30 minutes of your rental should be about getting comfortable, not pushing limits. Get used to the throttle response, the braking, the steering weight. These cars reward respect and smooth inputs. Learn the car before you ask anything of it.

4. Park Strategically

Avoid tight parking structures if possible. Wide, open parking lots with plenty of space to maneuver are your friend. Ask our team for parking recommendations in whatever area you are heading to — we know all the best spots that will not give you anxiety about door dings.

5. What to Inspect Before You Drive

  • Walk around the car with our team and confirm any pre-existing marks are noted
  • Understand the fuel type required (most exotics use premium 91+ octane)
  • Know where the fuel door release is before you need it
  • Understand how the parking brake and drive modes work
  • Confirm the emergency contact number for our team is in your phone

6. Enjoy Every Single Moment

After 10 years, Ali’s advice to every first-time renter is the same: slow down and be present. Notice the sound of the engine at idle. Feel the steering in your hands. Watch people’s reactions. These cars are extraordinary machines and they deserve to be appreciated fully. The best rentals are the ones where you truly experience the car, not just drive it fast.

Ferrari vs Lamborghini: Which Exotic Should You Rent?

Pacific Coast Highway opens up ahead of you. The sun is off the water. The only decision standing between you and an unforgettable day in Southern California is one of the most legendary rivalries in automotive history: Ferrari or Lamborghini? For decades, these two Italian titans have fought for supremacy on the world’s most glamorous roads — and now they await you right here in Orange County. At First Class Exotics, we’ve curated a fleet that lets you settle the debate yourself. But choosing between Maranello’s prancing horse and Sant’Agata’s raging bull deserves a moment of thoughtful consideration.

The Ferrari Experience: Refined Italian Royalty

To slip behind the wheel of a Ferrari is to inherit nearly a century of racing pedigree. Every detail — from the unmistakable rev of a flat-plane crankshaft to the buttery shift of a dual-clutch transmission — has been engineered to deliver precision, poise, and pure emotion. Models like the F8 Tributo and 488 GTB offer a near-telepathic connection between driver and machine, rewarding those who appreciate finesse over flash.

Ferrari is the choice for the executive heading to a curated dinner in Newport Beach, the couple celebrating an anniversary with a drive up Mulholland, or the connoisseur who wants to be admired without seeming to demand it. If your idea of luxury is sophisticated, understated power, an exotic car rental in Orange County featuring a Ferrari may be your perfect match. The brand whispers wealth — loudly enough to turn heads, quietly enough to keep its dignity.

The Lamborghini Experience: Theatrical, Unapologetic Power

Where Ferrari refines, Lamborghini roars. Founded out of a feud with Enzo Ferrari himself, Lamborghini was born to be louder, wilder, and more dramatic than its rival — and nothing has changed since. The Huracán’s naturally aspirated V10 doesn’t just propel you forward; it announces your arrival from three blocks away. It’s the sound that echoes off Laguna Beach canyon walls and rattles valet stands from Beverly Hills to La Jolla.

Driving a Lamborghini is theater. The scissor doors. The angular, fighter-jet silhouette. The neon-bright paint options that were made for California light. If you’re celebrating a birthday in LA, a bachelor weekend in San Diego, or simply want every head to turn on Rodeo Drive, Lamborghini delivers. When the goal is unforgettable — when subtlety is decidedly not the point — this is your car.

Performance, Price, and Practicality

On paper, the two brands trade blows. A Ferrari F8 Tributo produces 710 horsepower; a Lamborghini Huracán EVO delivers 631 from its high-revving V10. Both rocket from zero to sixty in under three seconds. Ferraris generally prioritize a more analytical, track-tuned driving feel, while Lamborghinis emphasize raw acceleration and sonic drama.

Rental rates for both brands are comparable, though specific models, seasonal demand, and rental duration all factor in. Lamborghinis tend to be slightly more accommodating for tall drivers and offer marginally more cargo space — useful if your weekend includes luggage for a Santa Barbara getaway or a night in Palm Springs. Ferraris, on the other hand, reward the driver who wants precision on every curve of Ortega Highway or the winding stretch of Pacific Coast Highway through Malibu.

Which Should You Choose?

Ask yourself what story you want your day to tell. If you envision a sophisticated experience filled with curated dinners, quiet confidence, and a connection to motorsport heritage, choose Ferrari. If you want maximum impact, maximum noise, and a machine that turns every valet stop into a moment, choose Lamborghini. There is no wrong answer — only the version of yourself you most want to embody for the next 24 or 48 hours.

Better yet, why choose at all? Many of our clients book both across a long weekend, experiencing the rivalry firsthand. An exotic rental in Southern California isn’t just transportation — it’s a statement, a memory, and for many, a gateway to ownership down the road.

The Dangers of Exotic Car Rentals — What Happened with the Porsche GT3 RS in LA and Why Safety Matters

The roar of a flat-six echoing down Pacific Coast Highway, the kiss of Pacific air through an open window, the unmistakable thrill of commanding 500-plus horsepower along the cliffs of Laguna Beach — exotic car rental is, at its finest, a sensory symphony reserved for those who appreciate the rarest things in life. But like any privilege, it carries responsibility. A recent high-profile incident involving a Porsche GT3 RS in Los Angeles has reignited an important conversation across the SoCal luxury community: when it comes to renting an exotic, safety, expertise, and partnership matter as much as horsepower.

What Happened with the Porsche GT3 RS in LA

The Porsche 911 GT3 RS is a track-bred masterpiece — a road-legal race car with active aerodynamics, downforce figures that rival prototypes, and a steering rack so communicative it feels telepathic. It is also, unequivocally, one of the most demanding machines ever sold to the public. When reports surfaced of a GT3 RS rental ending in a serious crash on the streets of Los Angeles, the story was tragically familiar: a driver unprepared for the car, a rental company unprepared to vet that driver, and a machine that responds harshly to overconfidence.

This wasn't simply a matter of speed. It was a matter of mismatch — the wrong car, the wrong moment, and the wrong support system. Across Los Angeles and Orange County, the exotic rental industry has exploded, and not every operator approaches the responsibility with the gravity it deserves. The result? Headlines that damage the entire industry and, far more importantly, put lives at risk.

Why Exotic Car Rentals Demand More Than a Credit Card Swipe

A Lamborghini Huracán, a Ferrari 296 GTB, a McLaren 720S — these aren't sports cars. They are precision instruments engineered for drivers who understand weight transfer, throttle modulation, and the consequences of mid-engine dynamics at speed. Yet too many rental outfits in Los Angeles and the greater SoCal region treat them like sedans at the airport counter. Hand over a license, sign a waiver, and you're off.

At First Class Exotics, we believe that approach is not only irresponsible — it's incompatible with luxury itself. True luxury is never reckless. It is curated, considered, and crafted around the client. Our clientele in Newport Beach, Irvine, and Costa Mesa expects more, and rightly so. A weekend cruise from the Pendry Newport Beach down to Montage Laguna Beach, or an evening arrival at Pelican Hill, should be defined by elegance, not anxiety.

How First Class Exotics Approaches Safety as a Standard, Not a Suggestion

Our philosophy begins with the client relationship. Every rental from First Class Exotics involves a thorough driver consultation — not as a formality, but as a conversation. We discuss your experience with high-performance vehicles, your planned routes, and the character of the car you're considering. If a client requests a track-focused machine like a GT3 RS, we ensure that the pairing is appropriate and that the driver is briefed on its nuances: the unforgiving aerodynamic balance, the carbon-ceramic brake temperatures, the razor-sharp throttle.

Our fleet is maintained to concours standards. Tires, brakes, fluids, and electronics are inspected before every handoff. Each delivery — whether to a private residence in Newport Beach, a yacht slip on Balboa Island, or a hotel valet in Los Angeles — includes a comprehensive walkaround and orientation.
