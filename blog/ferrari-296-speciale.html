Ferrari 296 Speciale — The Most Driver-Focused Ferrari in a Generation

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  8 min read  ·  June 2026

The Ferrari 296 Speciale is the kind of exotic rental that makes every corner feel meaningful. With a 3.0-liter twin-turbo V6 paired to an electric motor, this hybrid Ferrari delivers razor-sharp throttle response, surgical precision, and a level of engagement that is rare even among the most exotic cars in Orange County.

What sets the 296 Speciale apart

Ferrari built the 296 Speciale for drivers who want to feel the car instead of letting the car move them. It is lighter, more aerodynamic, and more connected than the standard 296 GTB. The electric boost adds immediate torque, while the combustion engine provides that signature Ferrari soundtrack as you climb toward redline.

A hybrid that still feels raw

Unlike most hybrids, the 296 Speciale is not designed to be quiet or invisible. It is designed to enhance response while preserving the Ferrari driving experience. The switchable driving modes let you choose between comfortable cruise mode and an aggressive track-ready setup that transforms the car into a highly composed canyon killer.

Why it is perfect for Southern California

This is the Ferrari for clients who want more than a glamorous arrival. It is ideal for an OC day that includes stops at Pelican Hill Resort, a walk along Newport Harbor, and a sunset run on Pacific Coast Highway. The car is comfortable enough for longer drives, yet sharp enough to make Ortega Highway and the canyon roads around Irvine feel like a private track.

Technical highlights every renter should know

  • 830 horsepower system output from the twin-turbo V6 and electric motor.
  • 0-60 mph in roughly 2.8 seconds with near-instant electric torque.
  • Rear-wheel steering and active aero for exceptional balance through fast curves.
  • Driver-first interior with a compact cockpit and premium Italian craftsmanship.

The ideal day with the 296 Speciale

Start with a pickup in Costa Mesa, head south to Laguna Beach for a coastal brunch, then let the car open up on the Pacific Coast Highway toward Dana Point. The hybrid system makes the driving experience feel effortless until you want it to feel ferocious. In the early evening, return through Newport Coast Drive and arrive at your hotel or private estate with a car that still looks razor-sharp after a full day of driving.
