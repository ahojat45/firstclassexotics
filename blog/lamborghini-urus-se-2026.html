2026 Lamborghini Urus SE — The Super SUV Just Got Deadlier

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  7 min read  ·  June 2026

The 2026 Lamborghini Urus SE is the rare SUV that behaves like a hypercar and feels at home on Pacific Coast Highway, Ortega Highway, and the fast boulevards of Orange County. First Class Exotics has been delivering dramatic Lamborghinis across SoCal for years, but the Urus SE brings a new level of aggression, technology, and comfort to the exotic SUV segment.

Why the Urus SE changes everything

The Urus SE is not a soft-luxury crossover. It is an 800-horsepower performance machine with active aerodynamics, rear-wheel steering, and a chassis engineered for corner exits that rival supercars. The driving position is commanding, the cabin is overtly premium, and the sound of the twin-turbo V8 is unmistakably Lamborghini.

For exotic car renters in Orange County, the Urus SE is the ideal choice when you want the Lamborghini statement without sacrificing practicality. It fits four adults plus luggage, it is capable in light rain, and it still delivers the head-turning presence we build our brand around.

The perfect luxury companion for OC

Orange County clients who book the Urus SE often choose it for long weekends that include multiple destinations. It is equally suited for a dinner at Pelican Hill Resort, a photoshoot on Newport Harbor, and a sunset cruise along the Pacific Coast Highway. Delivering a Lamborghini SUV to a hotel in Laguna Beach or a private estate in Newport Coast feels natural — and the Urus SE makes it effortless.

What makes the Urus SE a better rental option?

  • Power with control: 800 horsepower locked through all-wheel drive and an advanced torque-vectoring system.
  • Luxury with capability: heated and massaging seats, premium audio, and cargo room for weekend luggage.
  • Weather-ready: a performance SUV that can handle SoCal coastal mornings and light inland storms.
  • Attention-grabbing design: sharper lines, bronze accents, and an aggressive front splitter that create instant presence.

Best roads to drive the Urus SE

While the Urus SE is comfortable anywhere, it is most compelling on roads where you can stretch its legs. Pacific Coast Highway between Laguna Beach and Dana Point offers the perfect backdrop for its sharp handling and open-air drama. The wide curves of Newport Coast Drive allow you to feel the SUV’s composure. And if you want more intensity, Ortega Highway delivers enough sweepers to remind you this is still a Lamborghini at heart.

Why First Class Exotics recommends it

We recommend the 2026 Urus SE for clients who want the emotional drama of an Italian exotic with more everyday usability. It is especially popular for groups, corporate experiences, and luxury event transportation. If your day includes multiple stops — a hotel pickup, a photoshoot, and a restaurant arrival — the Urus SE is the exotic that keeps the entire experience together without compromise.
