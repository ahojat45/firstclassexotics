McLaren W1 — The Successor to the P1 Has Arrived

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  8 min read  ·  June 2026

The McLaren W1 is the car that picks up where the P1 left off. It is a hybrid hypercar tuned for maximum performance, extreme looks, and an experience that only a handful of drivers in Southern California will ever get to feel. The W1 is the new halo model for anyone who wants a rental that feels like an event from the moment it arrives.

Why the W1 matters

McLaren designed the W1 to be the most spiritually faithful successor to the P1, with 1,275 horsepower, an advanced aerodynamic package, and a chassis calibrated for both speed and agility. It is not just another limited-edition hypercar — it is a machine built to deliver a visceral experience on both road and track-inspired routes.

How it performs on SoCal roads

From the ribbon of Pacific Coast Highway to the canyon sweepers of Ortega Highway, the W1 feels at home wherever the road is open. Its hybrid powertrain provides instant acceleration, while the lightweight carbon fiber construction keeps the handling sharp and responsive. It is a car that rewards precision, but it also delivers enough drama to make every stoplight feel cinematic.

What makes the W1 the ultimate luxury rental

  • 1,275 horsepower hybrid drivetrain with instantaneous electric boost.
  • Active aerodynamics and track-grade suspension tuned for precision and stability.
  • Futuristic design that commands attention at Pelican Hill, Beverly Hills, or any VIP arrival.
  • Limited production rarity that makes a W1 rental feel like a once-in-a-lifetime event.

Planning your W1 day in Orange County

The W1 is perfect for clients who want to build the day around the car. Start with a delivery to Newport Harbor or a private residence in Laguna Beach, then head south on PCH to Dana Point for a coastal drive with the roof down. If you want a more intense route, Ortega Highway offers enough corners to remind you that the W1 is as capable as it is spectacular.
