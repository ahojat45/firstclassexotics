OC Car Culture — The Ultimate Guide to Exotic Cars in Orange County

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  9 min read  ·  June 2026

Orange County has quietly become one of Southern California’s most compelling exotic car cultures. From Cars & Coffee gatherings in Newport Beach to coastal runs on Pacific Coast Highway, OC offers a unique blend of luxury lifestyle, car passion, and scenic driving that is best experienced behind the wheel of a First Class Exotics rental.

Cars & Coffee and community

Every weekend, OC hosts some of the best car meets in the region. These gatherings are not just about cars — they are about community. Whether it’s the early-morning crowd in Newport Beach, a curated show near Fashion Island, or a laid-back meet in Irvine, these events are where collectors, enthusiasts, and exotic drivers connect.

Why the Pacific Coast Highway matters

PCH is Orange County’s automotive runway. The sweeping coastal views, the palm-lined turns, and the effortless pull of the road make it the perfect stage for a Lamborghini, Ferrari, or McLaren rental. The drugstore lines of Laguna Beach and the cliff-side approach to Dana Point are iconic, and the experience is enhanced when the car is as dramatic as the surroundings.

Newport Harbor and the prestige drive

Newport Harbor is where exotic cars and lifestyle converge. A harbor-side arrival in a Rolls-Royce Ghost or a Ferrari 296 Speciale is the kind of image that defines a luxury weekend in OC. The area is also a great place for photos, fine dining, and a relaxed cruise through some of Orange County’s most desirable neighborhoods.

The role of venues and delivery

First Class Exotics delivers to the most sought-after locations in Orange County: Pelican Hill Resort, Montage Laguna Beach, Pendry Newport Beach, and more. For event planners, hotel concierges, and VIP guests, the car is not just transportation — it is part of the event. A luxury exotic arrival at a coastal resort or a gated community instantly elevates the experience.

The best OC driving itinerary

  • Morning: Start with a Cars & Coffee gathering in Newport Beach.
  • Midday: Cruise down Pacific Coast Highway through Laguna Beach.
  • Afternoon: Stop at Pelican Hill Resort or Pendry Newport Beach for a scenic break.
  • Evening: Finish with harbor-side photos at Newport Harbor and a dinner arrival at a top OC restaurant.

Why exotic car culture is so strong here

Orange County offers a rare combination: beautiful roads, luxury lifestyle destinations, and a community that appreciates the craftsmanship behind modern exotics. It’s not just about speed. It’s about presentation, presence, and the way a world-class car elevates an entire day.
