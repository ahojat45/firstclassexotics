Porsche 911 GT3 RS 4.0 — The Last Naturally Aspirated Legend

By First Class Exotics Team  ·  7 min read  ·  June 2026

The Porsche 911 GT3 RS 4.0 is one of the final naturally aspirated icons from a generation that defined the brand’s most visceral era. For Southern California drivers who want an exotic rental with razor-sharp feedback, track-derived grip, and a wild 4.0-liter flat-six howl, the GT3 RS 4.0 is the perfect choice.

The emotion of a naturally aspirated GT3

There is an immediacy to the GT3 RS 4.0 that no turbocharged car can replicate. The throttle response is instant. The engine revs cleanly to 9,000 rpm. Every downshift and every apex feels deliberate. This is a Porsche built for drivers who want to feel the machine moving beneath them.

Precision that still feels dramatic

Despite its race-born technology, the GT3 RS 4.0 remains surprisingly usable on Southern California roads. Its carbon-ceramic brakes are confidence-inspiring, the aerodynamic wing generates massive downforce, and the steering is famously direct. It transforms Ortega Highway, Mulholland Drive, and the canyon roads around Irvine into a series of deliberate challenges.

Why this car works for Orange County clients

If you want the drama of an exotic rental but also crave a real driving experience, this is the car for you. The GT3 RS 4.0 is excellent for weekend escapes from Newport Beach to Palm Springs, high-speed canyon runs between Irvine and Lake Elsinore, and arrival-focused experiences at venues like Pelican Hill and Montage Laguna Beach.

What to expect from the rental

  • 520 horsepower naturally aspirated flat-six with race-inspired acoustics.
  • 0-60 mph in under 3 seconds with a chassis designed for corner exits.
  • Lightweight carbon fiber bodywork and track-grade suspension tuning.
  • An unmistakable presence that turns heads from Beverly Hills to Balboa Island.

The right itinerary for a GT3 RS 4.0 day

Start your morning with a delivery to a hotel or private residence in Newport Beach. Head inland toward Ortega Highway for an exhilarating run through canyon sweepers. Finish the day with a drive back along Pacific Coast Highway, where the GT3 RS 4.0’s soundtrack is amplified by ocean breezes and coastal light.
